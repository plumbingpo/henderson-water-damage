Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

Henderson Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Henderson and surrounding areas. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Every few years, ask a licensed plumber to look over the system for corrosion and worn parts
  • Aging galvanized or polybutylene plumbing should be swapped out ahead of an unexpected failure
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze

Why Burst Pipe Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.
